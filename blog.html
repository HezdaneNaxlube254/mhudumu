Understanding High Blood Pressure: A Guide for Kenyans

Dr. Peter Kipchoge March 15, 2024

High blood pressure, or hypertension, is often called the "silent killer" because it has no symptoms but can lead to serious health problems like heart attack and stroke. In Kenya, it's estimated that 1 in 4 adults has high blood pressure, and many don't even know it.

What is Blood Pressure?

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against the walls of your arteries. When this pressure is consistently too high, it puts strain on your heart and blood vessels, leading to damage over time.

Risk Factors for Kenyans

  • High salt intake (common in processed foods and some traditional dishes)
  • Low consumption of fruits and vegetables
  • Physical inactivity
  • Stress
  • Family history
  • Obesity

Prevention Tips

  • Reduce salt intake - use herbs and spices instead
  • Eat more fruits and vegetables - aim for 5 portions daily
  • Exercise regularly - 30 minutes of walking daily
  • Maintain a healthy weight
  • Limit alcohol consumption
  • Quit smoking
  • Manage stress through relaxation techniques

Local Foods That Help

  • Sukuma wiki (rich in potassium)
  • Bananas (excellent source of potassium)
  • Beans and lentils (fiber-rich)
  • Sweet potatoes (low glycemic index)
  • Fish (omega-3 fatty acids)

Remember: Regular check-ups are essential. Visit MHUDUMU Hospital for blood pressure screening and personalized advice from our cardiology team.
