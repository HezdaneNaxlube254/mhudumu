Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Contact Questions

What is the best number to call in an emergency?

For medical emergencies, please call our emergency line immediately: +254 722 000 000. This line is staffed 24/7 by trained emergency response personnel. For ambulance services, call +254 733 000 000.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through:

  • Our online booking form on the Appointment page
  • Calling our reception at +254 700 000 000
  • Visiting our main branch in Kitengela
  • Sending a WhatsApp message to +254 712 345 678
Do you offer telemedicine or virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer virtual consultations for follow-up appointments and non-emergency cases. Please call our reception to schedule a telemedicine appointment. You'll receive a video call link via WhatsApp or email.

How can I give feedback or file a complaint?

We value your feedback. You can:

  • Fill out the contact form on this page
  • Email our patient relations department at feedback@mhudumuhospital.co.ke
  • Call our patient support line at +254 700 555 666
  • Speak to our patient relations officer at any branch

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our team is ready to help you 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
