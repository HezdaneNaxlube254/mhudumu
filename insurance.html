FAQs

Insurance & Payment Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about insurance and payments

Do you accept all NHIF packages?

Yes, MHUDUMU Hospital is fully NHIF accredited and accepts all NHIF packages including Supa Cover, Linda Mama, and Civil Servants schemes. Simply present your NHIF card at registration. We process claims directly with NHIF so you don't have to worry about reimbursements.

How does direct billing work with insurance companies?

For patients with insurance from our partner providers (Jubilee, AAR, Britam, CIC, Resolution), we bill the insurance company directly. You only need to:

  • Present your insurance card at registration
  • Complete a claim form (we'll assist you)
  • Pay for any services not covered by your policy or any applicable co-pay/excess
What is the M-Pesa Paybill number and how do I use it?

Our Lipa Na M-Pesa Paybill is 123456. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to M-Pesa menu on your phone
  2. Select "Lipa Na M-Pesa"
  3. Select "Paybill"
  4. Enter Business Number: 123456
  5. Enter Account Number: Your Patient ID (ask at reception)
  6. Enter Amount and your M-Pesa PIN
  7. Confirm and complete transaction

Show the confirmation message at our reception desk.

Do you offer payment plans for expensive procedures?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for certain procedures and treatments including surgeries, maternity packages, and chronic care management. Please speak to our finance department to discuss:

  • Interest-free installment plans
  • Customized payment schedules
  • Deposit requirements
  • Corporate billing arrangements

Contact our finance team at +254 700 555 666 or visit our accounts office for a consultation.

How do I set up a corporate medical scheme for my company?

Setting up a corporate medical scheme is easy. Here's the process:

  1. Contact our corporate sales team at corporate@mhudumuhospital.co.ke or call +254 700 111 222
  2. We'll schedule a meeting to understand your company's healthcare needs
  3. We'll provide a customized proposal with pricing and benefits
  4. Once approved, we'll issue employee ID cards and setup accounts
  5. Your employees can start accessing services immediately

Corporate benefits include preferential rates, monthly invoicing, dedicated account manager, and wellness programs.

What if my insurance provider is not listed among your partners?

If your insurance provider is not currently on our partner list, you still have options:

  • Pay for services and claim reimbursement from your insurance company
  • We can provide all necessary documentation for your claim
  • Ask your insurance provider to contact us for possible direct billing arrangements
  • Some insurers may authorize single-case direct billing upon request

Our insurance desk can assist you with documentation and guidance. Contact +254 700 555 666 for help.

Are credit cards accepted and are there any additional charges?

Yes, we accept Visa and Mastercard payments at our finance office. Important information:

  • No additional charges for card payments - you pay exactly the bill amount
  • Both debit and credit cards accepted
  • Cards must be in the patient's name or accompanied by authorization
  • For large amounts, please inform your bank beforehand to avoid transaction blocks
What documents do I need for insurance claims?

For insurance claims, please bring the following documents:

  • Insurance card - Valid membership card
  • Identification - National ID or passport
  • Claim forms - Pre-filled if available (we can also provide)
  • Employer letter - For corporate schemes (if required)
  • Referral letter - If required by your insurance policy

Our insurance desk staff will guide you through the process and help with any missing documentation.
