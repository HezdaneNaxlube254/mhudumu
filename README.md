# 🏥 MHUDUMU HOSPITAL

![MHUDUMU Hospital](assets/images/hospital-building.jpg)

**Your Health, Our Sacred Duty** | *Afya Yako, Wajibu Wetu*

Official website for MHUDUMU Hospital, a modern healthcare facility serving Kitengela and surrounding areas since 2020. NHIF accredited provider offering 24/7 emergency services, maternity care, outpatient services, and specialized clinics.

## 📋 Pages

- **Home** – Hero section, key services, stats, insurance badges
- **About Us** – History, mission, vision, leadership team
- **Services** – All departments with detailed expandable sections
- **Doctors** – Staff grid with professional profiles
- **Insurance & Payments** – NHIF, insurance partners, payment methods
- **Contact** – Full contact form, map, branch locations
- **Book Appointment** – Online booking system
- **Health Blog** – Medical articles and health tips

## 🚀 Features

- **24/7 Emergency Header** – Sticky emergency contacts on all pages
- **NHIF Accredited** – Prominent badge and insurance information
- **Mobile-First Design** – Fully responsive for all devices
- **Online Booking** – Appointment form with validation
- **WhatsApp Integration** – Floating chat button
- **FAQ Accordion** – Collapsible questions and answers
- **Testimonials Carousel** – Patient reviews
- **Department Filter** – Browse services by category
- **Doctor Profiles** – Specialist information with credentials
- **Health Blog** – Regular medical articles

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Semantic markup
- **CSS3** – Custom styling with variables
- **JavaScript** – Interactive features
- **Font Awesome 6** – Icons
- **Google Fonts** – Inter font family
- **AOS Library** – Scroll animations
