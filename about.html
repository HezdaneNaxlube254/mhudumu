Our Story

Providing Quality Healthcare Since 2020

MHUDUMU Hospital began with a simple vision: to bring world-class healthcare to the Kitengela community. Founded in 2020 by a team of dedicated healthcare professionals, we've grown from a small clinic to a comprehensive medical facility serving thousands of patients annually.

The name "MHUDUMU" (meaning "service" in Swahili) reflects our commitment to serving our community with compassion, integrity, and excellence. Today, we're proud to be one of Kitengela's most trusted healthcare providers, offering everything from emergency care to specialized medical services.
